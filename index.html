SlotSync

Office hours scheduling for professors — configure your weekly blocks once, share one booking link, and let students request slots. Everything lives in the link itself: no server, no accounts, nothing leaves your browser.

1 · About you

Shown at the top of your booking page. The reply email receives students' booking requests.

2 · Weekly office-hour blocks

Each block repeats every week of the semester and is split into bookable slots of the chosen length. Add as many blocks as you like.

About locations & meeting links. SlotSync has no accounts and no calendar/Zoom/Teams/Meet integrations — there is no server to hold them. Instead, create a standing (recurring) meeting in Zoom, Teams, or Meet once, copy its link, and paste it here. SlotSync attaches the right link to every booking for that block. For in-person hours, just type the room.

3 · Semester & exceptions

Blackout dates

No office hours on these dates (holidays, conferences, travel). Pick a date and add it, or import your calendar below to get suggestions.

    Per-week overrides

    Cancel an entire week (fall break, conference travel) or move one block to a different day/time for a single week.

      Import your calendar (.ics) — optional

      Upload an export of your existing calendar (.ics from Google/Outlook/Apple). SlotSync scans it for events that collide with your blocks during the semester and suggests them as blackout dates. The file is parsed locally in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

      4 · Booking rules

      Fairness cap. It is shown to students and enforced against the claimed-slots list embedded in the link (there is no database, so the professor enforces it when confirming).

      5 · Share & export

      Your entire configuration is encoded into the booking link itself (#book=…) — the link is the database. Post it on your syllabus or LMS. If you change anything, copy a fresh link; old links keep the old schedule.

      How booking works without a backend. A student picks a slot and gets (1) a prefilled email to you with the slot details, (2) a calendar file for themselves. Nothing is recorded automatically — you confirm by replying. To make a taken slot disappear for future visitors, mark it as claimed below and re-share the updated link.

      Claimed slots (optional)

      After a student books and you confirm, add their slot here so the next person opening your link sees it as taken. Then copy and share the link again.
